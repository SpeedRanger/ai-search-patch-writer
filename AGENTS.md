# AGENTS.md

This folder is a public product surface for AI Search Patch Writer.

Rules:

- Keep the product static, public-safe, and easy to inspect.
- Do not add private paths, local machine details, tokens, API keys, secrets, or
  customer data.
- Do not claim Product Hunt rank, AI-search rank, citations, or revenue without
  evidence in `launch-status.json` or `launch-ledger.md`.
- Prefer concrete page patches over dashboards, scores, or vague AI SEO advice.
- Treat `llms.txt`, schema, and crawler controls as supporting artifacts, not
  magic ranking levers.
- Public issues may collect only public URLs and public page copy. Move any
  private delivery, payment, analytics, or customer-specific work to a
  buyer-approved private channel.
