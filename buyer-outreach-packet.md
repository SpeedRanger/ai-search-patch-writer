# Buyer Outreach Packet

> Product: AI Search Patch Writer
> Offer: `$149` AI-search patch pass for one public product page
> Revenue proof rule: do not count revenue until receipt/export/invoice evidence
> exists.

## Best-Fit Buyers

Use this only for public-safe outreach. Do not ask for private analytics, private
repos, secrets, customer data, or payment details in a public thread.

Best targets:

- B2B SaaS founders with public product pages but vague AI/SEO positioning.
- Devtool makers with docs/pricing/comparison pages that are hard for agents to
  parse.
- AI visibility/GEO founders who currently sell dashboards but lack patch
  examples.
- Local-service operators whose pages lack buyer-fit, proof, and comparison
  language.
- Product Hunt makers asking for launch-page/tagline feedback.

Bad targets:

- Heavily regulated health/legal/financial pages without private review.
- Companies requiring private analytics to judge the page.
- Teams asking for guaranteed AI citations or ranking.
- Products with no public page to patch.

## Public Comment

Use when replying to a public launch, roast, or feedback thread:

```text
Your page has enough product signal for a stronger AI-search patch. The gap is
not another visibility dashboard; it is public-page context that agents and
buyers can actually use: who it is for, when to choose it, when not to, proof,
comparison language, FAQ/schema, and crawlability notes.

I built AI Search Patch Writer for that exact pass:
https://speedranger.github.io/ai-search-patch-writer/

Public URLs only. No private analytics or secrets.
```

## Direct Message

Use only where direct outreach is appropriate:

```text
I noticed your product page is strong on the product idea but thin on the
public-page signals AI search systems and buyers use to compare tools: buyer
fit, alternatives, proof, pricing boundary, FAQ/schema, and crawlability.

I built a small tool that writes the patch pack:
https://speedranger.github.io/ai-search-patch-writer/

I’m opening 3 launch slots at $149 for one public page. You send a public URL;
I send back a page patch with comparison copy, FAQ/schema, proof checklist, and
rollback notes. No private analytics or secrets needed.
```

## Fast Qualification

Before offering the paid pass, check:

1. Does the buyer have a public product page?
2. Is the page missing buyer fit, comparison, proof, FAQ, schema, or
   crawlability clarity?
3. Can the patch be delivered without private data?
4. Would one concrete page patch plausibly save the buyer time or improve a
   live launch?
5. Can payment and delivery move to a buyer-approved private channel?

## Kill Criteria

Stop outreach for this product if:

- ten relevant makers see the offer and nobody asks for a patch;
- buyers ask only for guaranteed rankings/citations;
- every serious request requires private analytics before value is visible;
- the buyer reaction is "interesting dashboard" instead of "send me the patch."
